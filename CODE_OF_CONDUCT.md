# Code of Conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our commitment

This project is a place to work on hard problems together. Everyone is welcome regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or how many questions they ask.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. "This is wrong because …" beats "this is stupid".
- Assume the other person has context you do not.
- Give credit for ideas and reviews.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a good idea because it does not fit the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Pushing real patient data, or arguing to weaken the medical disclaimer or the red-flag gate.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, [@drunksu](https://github.com/drunksu), through a private
message or the contact details on that profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Responses may
range from a request to stop, to removing comments, to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on
severity and repetition. The maintainer will explain the decision to the people involved.
